Frequently asked questions

Answers before we begin.

What is included in the free audit?

We review your current Google presence and look for practical opportunities to improve your local visibility. You’ll receive clear initial observations, with no obligation to proceed.

How long does local SEO take to make a difference?

It depends on your location, competition and starting point. Local SEO is an ongoing process, so we focus on practical improvements and review available performance data over time rather than promise a specific ranking or timeline.

Do I need a website to improve my Google Business Profile?

A website isn’t required to set up a Google Business Profile, but a helpful, well-organised website can give potential customers more information and make it easier for them to contact you.

Which areas do you work with?

We’re focused on helping local businesses in Shropshire. Get in touch to discuss your location and the areas you serve.

Will I need to provide access to my business accounts?

For some agreed work, access to your Google Business Profile or website analytics may be useful. We’ll explain what’s needed first and only request access relevant to the work.
